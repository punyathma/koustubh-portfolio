My approach

How I build a community.

What seven years of building communities taught me, in plain words. It works for students, creators, fans or customers.

People move through a community in six steps.

Most of my work is figuring out what gets someone to the next one.

01
Join
How do the right people find you?
Through people they already trust. Members, fellows and creators bring in people who fit.
300+ colleges, ₹0 on ads
02
Take part
What gets someone to actually show up?
Give them one small, clear thing to do on day one.
350M+ organic views
03
Contribute
How do members start building with you?
Hand them a real role, not a task.
4,000-person crew ran a festival
04
Belong
When does it start feeling like theirs?
Rituals, recognition and leaders from within.
1,000+ fellows leading locally
05
Come back
Why would they return?
Something new to look forward to — perks, challenges, the next meetup.
500 → 1M app users
06
Bring others
When do they start inviting friends?
When they are proud of it — or when it pays them to.
₹25L+ paid to creators

What seven years taught me

Finding people

Ads bring people who leave. Friends bring people who stay.

Getting them to show up

Nobody joins something vague. Give them one clear, small thing to do first.

Getting them back

One great night isn't enough. People come back for the next thing on the calendar.

Making it theirs

It clicks when people start saying “we” usually after they’ve built something together.

Culture

Culture is just what you repeat: rituals, words, how you recognise people.

Online to in-person

Make meeting in person the reward for being online.

In-person to online

The event is the start, not the end. Get them on the app at the door.

Programs

Campaigns end. Programs come back every season.

Experiences

One good room does more than a hundred posts.

Growth

The best content about a community is made by the community.

Brands

A brand should add to the experience, not interrupt it.
