01/ What it was

A Community & Marketing IP

Summit at Campus was a half-day educational festival that took Under25 into colleges across India. It helped us reach new students, introduce them to the brand, bring them into our community, and build brand love through real-world experiences. Each campus became a new marketing and community touchpoint for Under25.

02/ What I did

Turned one show into a format anyone could run.

Wrote the playbook · built local crews of fellows · curated 800+ speakers and 500+ performers · Serviced brands like Zerodha got people onto the app at the door.

03/ What I learned

Write it down. Let locals run it. Make every show feed the next.

A written format travels. Local crews make it theirs. People, content and partners from one show bring in the next one.

04/ What it achieved

0 → 300+ shows. ₹10 Cr+ in sponsorships. ₹0 on ads.

300+ colleges including IITs, IIMs, SRCC, St. Xavier’s, Christ and NIFT. 24+ cities. 217K+ people reached.
