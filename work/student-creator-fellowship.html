01/ The job

Making students want to be a part of Under25

Built and ran 100+ hyperlocal Under25 Club pages across colleges, creating a network of student-run pages that helped bring Under25 programs, events and opportunities into individual campuses. The network now reaches 1L+ followers and publishes 350+ community-led posts every month.

02/ @Under25atcampus

Turning Community life into content

Made the page a notice board for the community celebrating students, launching programs and making people feel seen. Covered 300+ shows live, with at least 3 posts per show, ran 20+ campaigns, and published 4,000+ posts across student stories, challenges, skits, college collaborations, program launches and event coverage.

03/ @under25irl

Building smaller communities around shared interests

Built the page for curated IRL experiences and niche communities. 

Grew it to 4.4K followers, with 163 posts, 50+ student collaborations and content across 5+ communities creators, first-time jobbers, athletes, and musicians.

04/ What it achieved

Making community a growth channel

Grew @Under25Campus to 26K and @Under25IRL to 4.4K, while the Club network crossed 1L+ followers. 

Built a content engine producing 350+ community posts every month, with the highest-viewed video crossing 52M views.
